# Decisions made without the owner

Claude made these choices while working through the launch checklist unattended, for items that were only *softly* blocked (a product choice with a sensible default). Each one is easy to change. Branding, business identity, legal and money decisions were **not** made here; they stay in Phase 0 of [LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md](LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md).

To change a decision, tell Claude "change decision N" or edit the setting mentioned.

| # | Date | Item | Decision | Why | How to change it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-12 | C26 Help pages | Support email, phone, response time and Instagram/TikTok/Facebook links are **settings in Admin → Settings → Support and social accounts**, empty by default. Empty fields are hidden; the Contact page says the support address "will be listed here soon" until it is set. | The support channel (D7) and social accounts (P8) aren't decided yet, so nothing is invented; the pages work as soon as you fill the fields. | Fill or clear the fields in Admin → Settings. |
| 2 | 2026-10-12 | C26 FAQ text | The FAQ describes today's behaviour: unused photos deleted after 24 h, kept photos and videos stay until the customer deletes them, credits don't expire, no refunds, free redo via reports within 7 days, 3 refusals block the account. | It must match the Terms and the code. | When D11 (how long files are kept) is decided, update the "How long do you keep my photos?" answer in `components/help-pages.tsx` with the Privacy Policy. |
| 3 | 2026-10-12 | C27 What you receive | The template page says results come **with no watermark**, as MP4 at the template's resolution. | That is what the app delivers today. | If you decide on watermarks for free results (D16/V41), change the line in `components/template-detail.tsx`. |
| 4 | 2026-10-12 | C7 Error tracking | Errors are **always** stored in the app (Admin → Operations → Recent errors, 30 days). **Sentry is optional**: paste a DSN in Admin → Connections → Error tracking. Errors go to Sentry over its plain HTTP API, with no extra package. | Works on day one without another account, and Sentry's free plan covers alerts to email/phone when you want them (I13). No SDK keeps the Worker small. | Add or remove the DSN in Connections. |
| 5 | 2026-10-12 | C4.1 Admin two-step sign-in | Two-step sign-in is **available to every account** but **not forced**; admins see a reminder banner on every admin page until they turn it on. Setup shows the key and an `otpauth://` link instead of a QR code. | Forcing it could lock the only admin out before launch; the banner keeps it visible. A QR code needs an extra package, and the key works in every authenticator app. | Turn it on in Account → Two-step sign-in. If you want it forced for admins, ask Claude to require it before admin pages open. |
| 6 | 2026-10-12 | Dates on pages | All dates shown by the shared date helper use the **Europe/Tirane** time zone. | Server and browser printed different dates around midnight and the page failed to load scripts correctly (hydration error). The studio's customers are in Albania. | Change the `timeZone` in `components/shared.tsx`. |
| 7 | 2026-10-12 | C10 Default language | The site opens in **Albanian**; visitors switch to English with the Shqip/English switch (footer and mobile menu), remembered for a year. Emails go out in the language the customer last used. | The customers are in Albania. | Change `DEFAULT_LANG` in `lib/i18n.ts`. |
| 8 | 2026-10-12 | C10 Admin language | The studio admin (pages, admin API messages, the admin's own emails) **stays in English**. Template names, descriptions, photo tips and custom guideline lines show exactly as the admin writes them, so write them in Albanian. | Only you use the admin; translating it doubles the review work in P7. | Ask Claude to translate the admin too. |
| 9 | 2026-10-12 | V10 Support role | **Support** can open Users, Generations, Reports and Operations; send reset links, mark emails verified, unblock refusals, retry failed videos and resolve reports (approving a report gives a free redo). Support **cannot** see the dashboard's money, adjust credits, reverse purchases, export CSVs, edit templates, packs, connections, providers or settings, suspend/delete accounts or change roles. | A support person should help customers without being able to move money or break the studio. Resolving reports includes free redos because that's the everyday support job. | Change `supportMay` in `lib/server/api.ts` and `SUPPORT_SECTIONS` in `lib/contracts.ts`. |
| 10 | 2026-10-12 | V3 Share links | A shared video's page **stays online until the customer stops sharing or deletes the video** (no expiry). Share pages are not indexed by search engines. | Customers post these links on social media, where a 23-hour link breaks; revoking is one tap. | Add an expiry in `lib/server/share.ts` if you prefer. |
| 11 | 2026-10-12 | V26 Video-ready notifications | Notifications work **while the site is open in any tab** (including in the background). Notifications to a fully closed browser (Web Push) are not built. | Web Push needs VAPID keys and a push service set up on your side; the email (C11.2) already covers customers who closed the site. | Ask Claude to add Web Push once you want it. |
| 12 | 2026-10-12 | V30 Removal requests | A request that includes a share link **hides that shared page immediately**, before anyone reviews it. Removing the video deletes it from the owner's account (with an email to them); it is not a strike against the owner's account. Each new request also appears under Operations → Recent errors so it gets noticed quickly. | Fast takedown protects the person shown; hiding a share page is harmless if the request turns out wrong (the owner can share again). | Change `createRemovalRequest`/`resolveRemoval` in `lib/server/removals.ts`. |
| 13 | 2026-10-12 | V6/V40 Community gallery | Videos appear publicly **only when the owner chooses "Share to the community"** (with a reminder that everyone in the video must agree). Public videos show on a new **Community** page (header link) and on their template's page. Likes need an account. Staff can hide any video. | Face videos are personal data; opt-in keeps it safe and matches the Privacy Policy. | Hide the Community link or remove the page if you don't want a public feed. |
| 14 | 2026-10-12 | V42 Review requests | After a 4- or 5-star rating the customer sees a small "leave a review" link, **only if** you set a review page link in Admin → Settings → Support and social accounts. No emails are sent for this. | Asking only happy customers, once, at the right moment, without spamming. | Clear the review link to switch it off. |
| 15 | 2026-10-12 | V5 Provider webhooks | Video status still comes from **polling** (the every-minute dispatcher, plus quicker checks while My creations is open), not provider webhooks. | Higgsfield (the main provider) is polled through its status API, which already works and is tested; webhooks would add another public endpoint and secret to set up before launch, for at most a minute of speed. | Ask Claude to add provider webhooks after the first real runs (C32.8) if videos feel slow to appear. |
